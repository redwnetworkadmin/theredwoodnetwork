TikTok LIVE, explained

Connect, entertain, and earn in real time.

TikTok LIVE lets eligible creators broadcast live, talk directly with viewers, build fan communities, and access features such as virtual Gifts and subscriptions where available.

1

Start a LIVE

Eligible creators launch a real-time stream directly through TikTok and choose a topic, format, or activity.

2

Engage Viewers

Creators respond to comments, welcome new viewers, host guests, and turn passive followers into an active community.

3

Receive Support

Eligible viewers can send virtual Gifts. TikTok may use Diamonds to recognize creator popularity and contribution.

4

Build Momentum

Consistent streams, stronger retention, and better community habits can help creators deepen their presence over time.

TikTok LIVE access, Gifts, subscriptions, Diamonds, and other monetization features depend on age, location, account standing, and TikTok eligibility requirements. Redwood Network is an independent creator network and is not TikTok.

Questions

Know before you apply.

Every creator is different. These answers provide a basic overview of Redwood Network and TikTok LIVE.

What is Redwood Network?

Redwood Network is a creator network focused on helping TikTok LIVE creators improve their strategy, community, consistency, and access to future opportunities.

Do I need a large following?

Not necessarily. We care about potential, consistency, professionalism, content quality, and your willingness to improve—not just follower count.

How old do I need to be to go LIVE?

TikTok currently states that creators must be at least 18 years old to go LIVE. Other LIVE features may have additional location and eligibility requirements.

How do creators earn through LIVE?

Eligible creators may receive virtual Gifts, collect Diamonds, offer subscriptions, participate in LIVE shopping, or pursue outside brand partnerships. Availability and requirements vary.

Is joining Redwood guaranteed to increase earnings?

No. Results depend on the creator, audience, content, consistency, market conditions, platform rules, and many other factors. Redwood provides support and opportunities but does not guarantee results.

Are there contracts or streaming quotas?

No. Redwood Network does not require long-term contracts or mandatory streaming quotas. Creators are expected to communicate, participate professionally, and stay active enough to benefit from the network.

Does Redwood offer prizes or incentives?

Yes. Redwood may offer cash prizes, creator tournaments, internal challenges, and performance-based incentives depending on the active program or event.

How can a brand work with Redwood?

Brands can submit an inquiry describing their product, campaign goals, budget, dates, and ideal creator audience. Our team can then explore potential creator matches.
